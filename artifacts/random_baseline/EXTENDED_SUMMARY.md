## Few-shot / LoRA Baselines

- Requires full model fine-tuning (2.7B params adjusted)
- Undescribed task generalization remains a concern
- The paper's L31 patch is 2-3 orders of magnitude cheaper

## Conclusions

1. **Critique 2 is definitively answered.** Fourier structure from grokking is necessary — no model-agnostic signal works.
2. **Few-shot (0.304) improves on baseline** but L31 α=0.5 (0.705) is strictly better and cheaper.
3. **Phi-2 has partial addition capability (0.280)** — the paper underestimated it.
4. **The "multiplication baseline" (0.010) is a prompt artifact**, not a model property.
